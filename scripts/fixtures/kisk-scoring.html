How we score

A piece's grade is how close its lines come to what the best players of your class wear on that slot. Each line counts the share of your class's top players in Korea who carry it there, and the piece is scored against the best piece the game has for its slot.

The grade

line = the share of top players who carry it on this slot × its roll ÷ the slot's largest roll
score = the piece's lines ÷ the best piece of its slot

Each line on a card shows its share: Move Speed on boots shows 100%, since every top player has it there. A line counts all of its share at the largest roll any item of the slot has, so the same line on a lower item counts for less. A score of 100 is the best piece in the game, and any two pieces for a slot compare directly, whatever their level or grade.

Nothing is projected. A line that isn't fully bound counts at its size now, and an Arcana counts the skills and levels it has.

The letters come from the gear of global players with a top-tenth parse on a group boss in Abyss Logs: a quarter of their pieces are below B, half below A, three quarters below S and nine in ten below S+. BiS is within a tenth of the best piece in the game, which none of them has yet.

Why Korea

Korea's servers are the oldest, so their top players have finished rerolling: Move Speed is on the boots of every top player there, and of only 21% of global players with a top-tenth parse.

The shares come from cielui, which counts the soul-binding lines of Korea's ranked PvE players from NC's own armory every day. These were taken on 2026-10-19, over 528 to 1.2k players a class. It lists the eight lines and eight skills each slot carries most, so anything rarer counts as nothing.

Upgrades

A character's Upgrades tab says what it takes to bring each piece to S+. For gear, that's rerolls: each names a line to reroll and what to keep rerolling it until it lands, and any of those, with the piece's other rerolls, brings it to S+. Of the ways to get there, it takes the one with the fewest tries on average.

Each piece gets a card with the range it ends in, from the worst luck to the best, its rerolls in order, each pointing to the best stats to reroll for, and all the stats to reroll for in tiers by how they compare with the best of them. The cards go by what a try adds to their piece's score over all of its rerolls, the most first. Lines count once fully bound, but bind a piece fully only once its rerolls land: a line rerolled after that has to be bound again. Rerolls that only land skills don't wait, since binding doesn't change a skill line.

An Arcana can't be rerolled. It's enchanted, or a new Unique is crafted with the slot's two most carried skills, whichever ends higher on average once fully enchanted. A craft costs far more than a reroll, so crafts get a list of their own.

What it leaves out

  • Patches. Korea is a few patches ahead, and its players change their gear when a patch changes a skill. Chanters there moved Earth's Promise off their armor after it was weakened in August, so it grades low on armor here whether or not the global servers have that change yet.
  • Your own character. A share is the same for every character of a class, so a line counts the same whether you're short of its stat or already have plenty, and whether or not it takes a skill to a specialization.
  • PvP. The shares are for PvE gear, and PvP players carry far more resists.
